Ken Blaskowitz

Author of Dark Botanical & Gothic Coloring Books

Exploring the macabre and the beautiful, my work draws inspiration from the intricate elegance of Victorian botanical engravings. I design dark academia and gothic coloring books for adults, each containing 40 meticulously crafted illustrations. These volumes invite you to unravel the mysteries of a forgotten, atmospheric world.
