Ken Blaskowitz

Illustrator & Designer of Gothic Coloring Books

Step into an intricate, atmospheric world where shadows dance with nature. Drawing inspiration from Victorian botanical engravings and celestial mysteries, I design coloring books that merge the beautiful with the dark, inviting adult colorists to lose themselves in slow, meditative artistry.

Featured Volume

The Victorian Poison Garden

A Dark Botanical Coloring Book for Adults

Some plants are beautiful precisely because they can kill you. Immerse yourself in 40 intricate illustrations of deadly plants like Belladonna, Hemlock, and Foxglove, drawn in the rich tradition of 19th-century apothecary botanicals. Designed for adult colorists who prefer their nature with a dark, exquisite edge.
